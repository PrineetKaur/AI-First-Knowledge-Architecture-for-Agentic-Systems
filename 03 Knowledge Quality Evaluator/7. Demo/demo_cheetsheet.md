# Demo & Walkthrough: Knowledge Quality Evaluator

## Purpose of this demo

This document walks you through how AI-ready knowledge and workflow assets can be evaluated using a consistent quality framework.

The goal is to show:

- Why structured assets still require quality governance
- How objective evaluation replaces subjective reviews
- How quality assessment supports continuous improvement of AI-ready knowledge systems

---

## Suggested demo flow (2–3 minutes)

### 1. Start with the problem (30–40 seconds)

Show:

`problem/measuring_ai_knowledge_quality.md`

Explain:

- Structured knowledge and workflows improve AI reliability
- However, structure alone does not guarantee quality
- As knowledge systems grow, inconsistencies become difficult to identify manually

Give a simple example:

A workflow may:

- Define every required step
- Still contain ambiguous decision logic

Or a knowledge asset may:

- Be well structured
- Omit an important business constraint

👉 **Key point to emphasise:**

> "Creating AI-ready assets is only the first step. Organizations also need a consistent way to evaluate whether those assets are reliable enough for production."

---

### 2. Explain the quality framework (40–50 seconds)

Show:

`design/what_is_an_evaluation_framework.md`

Explain:

The evaluation framework introduces a consistent way to assess AI-ready assets across multiple quality dimensions.

Highlight that quality reviews should be:

- Structured
- Repeatable
- Measurable
- Independent of individual reviewers

Then briefly mention the evaluation principles:

- Objective criteria
- Explicit scoring
- Reusable methodology
- Continuous improvement

👉 **Key pointer:**

> "Instead of asking whether documentation looks good, we ask whether it can reliably support AI systems."

---

### 3. Show the evaluation schema (30–40 seconds)

Show:

`schemas/quality_evaluation_schema.yaml`

Explain:

The schema standardizes how evaluation results are represented.

It captures:

- Evaluation metadata
- Dimension scores
- Overall quality score
- Findings
- Improvement recommendations

Highlight that:

- Every assessment follows the same structure
- Evaluation records become reusable governance assets

👉 **Key pointer:**

> "Just as schemas standardize knowledge and workflows, this schema standardizes quality assessment."

---

### 4. Walk through a quality assessment (40–50 seconds)

Show:

`quality/knowledge_asset_quality_assessment.json`

Explain how the assessment records:

- Quality dimensions
- Individual scores
- Reviewer observations
- Improvement opportunities
- Overall recommendation

Highlight that:

- Evaluation is evidence-based
- Quality can be tracked over time
- Reviews become comparable across assets

👉 **Key pointer:**

> "Quality becomes measurable rather than subjective."

---

### 5. Show governance and quality reviews (30–40 seconds)

Show:

`governance/quality_review_guidelines.md`

Then show:

`evaluation/quality_metrics.md`

Explain:

- Governance defines how reviews should be performed
- Quality metrics ensure every reviewer evaluates assets consistently
- Evaluation results feed continuous improvement rather than one-time approval

👉 **Key pointer:**

> "Governance transforms quality assessment from an occasional review into an ongoing operational process."

---

### 6. Close with impact (20–30 seconds)

Summarize:

We moved from:

- Structured knowledge
- Structured workflows

To:

- Structured quality governance

The result is:

- Objective quality measurement
- Consistent reviews
- Continuous improvement
- Greater confidence in AI-ready assets
- Stronger governance across the knowledge lifecycle

👉 **Key pointer:**

> "Reliable AI systems require more than well-structured knowledge—they require governed knowledge that can be measured, reviewed, and continuously improved."
