# Evaluation Summary

This document summarizes the evaluation results for the AI-ready assets reviewed as part of this subproject.

Rather than evaluating assets subjectively, the evaluation framework applies consistent quality criteria to determine whether knowledge and workflow assets are suitable for reliable AI agent use.

## Key Strengths

Across both assets, the evaluation identified several strengths:

- Explicit representation of business rules and decision logic.
- Consistent structure aligned with reusable schemas.
- Clear ownership and governance metadata.
- Modular design that supports reuse across AI agents.
- High suitability for retrieval, reasoning, and workflow execution.

These characteristics improve the reliability, predictability, and maintainability of AI-ready knowledge systems.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

Although both assets meet the quality threshold for production readiness, several opportunities for future improvement were identified:

- Expand edge-case coverage with additional examples.
- Introduce more detailed exception handling for complex operational scenarios.
- Broaden retrieval metadata to improve semantic search performance.
- Continue refining evaluation criteria as new asset types are introduced.

Continuous evaluation ensures that knowledge quality improves over time rather than remaining static.

---

## Overall Assessment

Both evaluated assets satisfy the quality standards defined by the evaluation framework.

They demonstrate:

- Structured and explicit design.
- Consistent application of architectural principles.
- Strong governance practices.
- High readiness for AI agent consumption.

Based on the evaluation results, both assets are considered suitable for production use within the scope of this project.
